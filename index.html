About

I'm Yuxuan Su, a master's student at the School of Social Sciences, Tsinghua University in Beijing, and I will start my Ph.D. here in 2027, working on AI for social science. I did my undergraduate degree here too, in History (B.A., 2025, GPA 3.97/4.0).

Alongside research, I run Entropy Order (ENTRO AI), a company that provides AI alignment data to LLM developers. We are backed by YC China, a well-known USD/RMB dual-currency fund, and reached about US$4 million in ARR, with frontier AI labs in China and abroad among our customers. The company is closely connected to my work on post-training data.

Research

AI for social science. I study concrete ways in which AI can accelerate discovery in the social sciences, and the conditions under which AI-assisted research is valid and reliable. My work so far covers LLM-based structuring and annotation of large corpora of political and historical texts, statistical inference that remains valid under run-to-run variability in model outputs, and multi-agent simulation for policy research.

Post-training data and environments. I study how post-training data and training environments affect model capability, in particular whether they extend what a model can do or only make existing capabilities more reliable. The goal is to characterize the boundary between the two, identify which kinds of data, tasks, and feedback signals move it, and develop ways to measure the difference.

我是苏宇轩，在清华大学社会科学学院读硕士，2027 年入学读博，方向是 AI for social science。本科也在清华，读的是历史学（2025 年毕业，GPA 3.97/4.0）。

研究

AI for social science。研究 AI 加速社会科学发现的具体路径，以及 AI 辅助研究在什么条件下有效、可靠。目前的工作包括：用大模型对大规模政治与历史文本做结构化和标注；在模型输出存在随机波动时保证统计推断的有效性；面向政策研究的多智能体模拟。

后训练数据与环境。研究后训练数据和训练环境如何影响模型能力，重点是它们究竟拓展了模型能做到的事，还是只让已有能力更稳定。目标是刻画二者的边界，找出哪些类型的数据、任务和反馈信号能推动这一边界，并发展相应的度量方法。
