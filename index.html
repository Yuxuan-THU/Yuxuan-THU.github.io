About

I'm Yuxuan Su, a researcher at Tsinghua University in Beijing, and I will start my Ph.D. here in 2027, working on AI for social science. I did my undergraduate degree here too, in History (B.A., 2025, GPA 3.99/4.0). Alongside research, I run Entropy Order, a company that provides AI alignment data to LLM developers. We're at roughly US$4 million in ARR and work with frontier AI labs in China and abroad. The company and the first research question below are closely connected.

Away from the desk I like sports. I've run a half marathon in 1 hour 30 minutes (April 16, 2025), and I can do 27 overhand-grip pull-ups in one set (May 31, 2026).

Research

My work currently follows two lines.

What post-training data and environments can add to a model. Better data and better training environments clearly improve models, but it is less clear when they push the edge of what a model can do and when they only polish what it could already do. I want to understand where that boundary sits, what kinds of data, tasks, and feedback move it, and how to tell the two cases apart.

How AI can speed up discovery in social science. I'm interested in the practical route, not the slogan: what has to be true for an AI tool to help with real research rather than produce plausible-looking papers. So far this has meant three kinds of work: using LLMs to structure and annotate large archives of political and historical text; making statistical inference hold up when model outputs fluctuate from run to run; and multi-agent simulation for policy research.

Earlier on, I also studied how Chinese LLMs are shaped by regulation and how deployment choices change the values a model expresses. That work is in the publications below.

研究

目前主要做两件事。

后训练数据和环境，能给模型带来什么。更好的数据、更好的训练环境确实能让模型变强，但它们什么时候是在推动能力边界，什么时候只是把模型本来就会的东西打磨得更顺，这一点并不清楚。我想弄明白这条边界在哪里，什么样的数据、任务和反馈能把它往外推，以及怎么区分这两种情况。

AI 怎样加速社会科学的发现。我关心的是具体能走通的路，而不是口号：AI 工具要满足什么条件，才能真正帮上研究，而不是写出一篇看起来像样的论文。到目前为止，做过三类事：用大模型对大规模政治与历史文本做结构化和标注；让统计推断在模型输出每次都有波动的情况下依然成立；以及面向政策研究的多智能体模拟。

更早的时候，我还研究过中国大模型如何受到监管的塑造，以及部署方式的不同如何改变模型表达出的价值取向。这部分工作见下面的论文。
